Gold Annotation Workbench

A guided example of the Excel review screen. Read the note. Capture exact evidence. Let the workbench build the records.

You only make evidence decisions

You do not choose a required category or subcategory. You do not build spreadsheet rows. You read the note in Notepad, copy the exact phrase into Selected source text, then choose what that phrase represents.

Source first: preserve the note’s own wording. Orthopedic surgeon is a valid context record. The optional open label may be blank. Client groupings such as medical or legal are compared later; they do not constrain this Gold review.

7. Seal Gold, then review any watchlist pair

After all notes in the claim have been read, seal the Gold annotation. Only then import client output and load matches that cite the current note. Use the note and your Gold evidence to decide whether the system entity is the watchlist candidate.

What you compare
Source note: Northstar Orthopedics is located at 100 Lake Avenue, Denver, CO 80202.

Client system entity: Northstar Orthopedics, 100 Lake Ave.
Watchlist candidate: Northstar Ortho Group, 910 Market Street.

The score is a candidate signal. The decision comes from the note evidence.
