How to annotate the Excel workbook

The notes and the workbook are the work. This page only demonstrates how to read the notes, find each reference, and fill the correct Excel cells.

What the coordinator gives you

  • One complete claim packet: every supplied UTF-8 note, such as C104_N01.txt and C104_N02.txt. Read every note before finalizing links.
  • One Excel workbook: entity-evaluation-batch-template.xlsx. Every source and review sheet contains claim_number; note-level sheets also contain note_id, so filtering keeps work focused.
  • This tutorial. It does not collect answers, create files, or replace the workbook. Save your work in Excel.

Release order: silver review can start early. Blind gold work is completed and sealed before the same claim’s client output and watchlist sheets are released to that reviewer. The original note text is always the source.

1. Read the whole claim before filling Excel

Claim C104 has two notes. She, her, and The firm only make sense in the complete claim. Click the highlighted words to follow the reasoning.

C104_N01.txt · note order 1

Maya Chen called. She said Elena Ruiz of Harbor Legal represents her.

C104_N02.txt · note order 2

Harbor Legal emailed Maya Chen. Harbor Legal requested a reply. The firm asked Elena Ruiz to respond.
Click a highlighted reference.
Every highlighted phrase becomes its own mention row, even where it refers to the same person or organization.

The two occurrences of “Harbor Legal” in N02 are two source locations. “The firm” is a description, not a new company. Its referent needs an explicit decision.

3. Fill the gold annotation workbook

Gold work means independently recording what the complete claim contains. Do not look at client records, watchlist candidates, system outputs, or another reviewer’s workbook.

Step 2A — enter one source reference at a time

N01: Maya Chen called. She said Elena Ruiz of Harbor Legal represents her.
N02: Harbor Legal emailed Maya Chen. Harbor Legal requested a reply. The firm asked Elena Ruiz to respond.
Click a phrase above. Its exact row in the Mentions sheet will light up.

Step 2B — fill the entity index after linking mentions

This claim has three local references: E1 Maya Chen, E2 Elena Ruiz, and E3 Harbor Legal. Local handles do not establish identity across claims.

Workbook order: fill Mentions; then Entities using the same entity_ref; then give every note a completion status in Review_log. Leave uncertainty unresolved and state candidates in reason.

3. Review the client’s entity records in Excel

Use this after the client’s raw rows are copied into 04_Client_entity_output. Do not change them. The raw sheet contains claim_number | note_id | entity_record | Identification_type, plus a coordinator-added record ID. Do not change it. Fill 05_Silver_entity_review after blind gold is sealed.

Step 3A — find the client row’s evidence in the original text

N02: Harbor Legal emailed Maya Chen. Harbor Legal requested a reply. The firm asked Elena Ruiz to respond.
Click a highlighted phrase. Start with repeated exact-search rows and a single GenAI entity row.

Step 3B — fill one review row for every client row

Use sealed gold mention IDs as evidence IDs, then enter them in 05_Silver_entity_review. This preserves the raw client row and creates traceability.

Why R03 and R04 do not get “occurrence 1” and “occurrence 2”: the exact-search rows have no position. Both are supported by both candidate passages, but source order would be invented provenance. Use supported_multiple_locations, list both evidence IDs, and explain why. R11 is one GenAI row supported by two names and “The firm”; it remains one client row.

4. Review the watchlist match against the claim evidence

The unaltered candidate data is in 06_Watchlist_pair_input. It includes claim number, watchlist name and record, system name and record, and similarity score. Preserve the sheet; fill 07_Watchlist_field_review and 08_Watchlist_pair_review. Inspect full notes and sealed gold evidence before deciding whether the match represents the watchlist entity in this claim.

Step 4A — compare each field before looking at the score

Watchlist: Harbor Legal

System: Harbor Legal
category: legal · address: blank · city: blank · state: blank · ZIP: blank · phone: blank · TIN: blank

In 07_Watchlist_field_review, mark name and category agrees. Mark blank system fields missing. Missing values do not conflict with supplied values.

Step 4B — make the pair decision

Choose insufficient_evidence for this pair. A perfect similarity score compares strings; it does not prove real-world identity. The summary sheet calculates accepted-match precision from determinate, included pair decisions.

pair_idfieldwatchlist valuesystem valuecomparisonreview note
Boundary: this pair dataset has no note ID, quotation, candidate-selection log, or threshold outcome. It supports record-to-record identity review only. It cannot prove source extraction or missed alerts. If notes are supplied, use the source-evidence fields in 08_Watchlist_pair_review; otherwise choose not_checked for source verification.

5. Check the Excel file before handoff

Save the completed Excel workbook with the coordinator’s required filename and return it through the agreed channel. A second reviewer works independently; a lead adjudicates in a separate copy.
